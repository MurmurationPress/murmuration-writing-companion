# About Murmuration Press smoke test

1. Open the Command Palette and run **About Murmuration Writing Companion**. Confirm one modal opens and identifies **Murmuration Writing Companion** with the installed manifest version.
2. Open **Settings → Murmuration Writing Companion** and activate **Open About** under **About Murmuration Writing Companion**. Confirm it opens the same modal without changing the diagnostic toggle or any other setting.
3. Confirm the description covers manuscript structure, editorial review, continuity, temporal awareness and Story World tools, followed by the concise Murmuration Press relationship and PRIME Trilogy support wording.
4. Use mouse and keyboard to activate the Murmuration Press website, **Purchase the PRIME Trilogy**, GitHub, Documentation and MIT licence controls. Confirm each opens the expected HTTPS page in the system browser and the purchase action is visually prominent without relying on colour alone.
5. Tab through every control at default and increased zoom. Confirm focus is visible, accessible labels describe each destination and modal reading order remains logical.
6. Repeat in light and dark themes and with a narrow Obsidian window. Confirm text wraps, controls remain readable and no horizontal scrolling obscures content.
7. Open and close the modal several times with Escape and the close control. Confirm no vault file, frontmatter, Story World data, plugin setting or workspace content changes.
8. Test while offline or with a deliberately unavailable destination. Confirm browser/link failure does not affect writing, indexing, manuscript navigation or plugin loading.
